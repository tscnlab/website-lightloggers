# Data sources

`library.json` is the canonical, curated inventory used by both Quarto notebooks.
`search-audit.json` contains the dated search protocol, accounting and other
candidate decisions. `search-decisions.csv`, `title-triage.csv`, `validations.csv`
and `references.bib` are reproducible exports.

`search/2026-11-10/*titles.json` contains the three complete final Europe PMC
responses. `title-shortlist.json` records the original title-prefilter output.
The other three broad search responses are capped pilot searches, not complete
retrievals. See the audit for their limitation.

Earlier research fragments, device-illustration notes and venue-asset notes are
historical working records. They can disagree with later verified corrections.
The current library merges LYS with LYS Button, restricts the Pilehchi-Ha CCT
endpoint to lido/OcuWEAR, and labels access and evidence types explicitly.
Device illustration metadata is not used or displayed on the website.

No source PDF or full article is redistributed in this bundle. Citation metadata,
source URLs and concise original paraphrases support inspection of the originals.
