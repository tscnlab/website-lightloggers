## ENLIGHT logo

`enlight-logo.png` is an unchanged download of the logo linked by the official [ENLIGHT statement website](https://enlight-statement.org/).

- Direct source: https://enlight-statement.org/logo.png
- Retrieved: 11 November 2026
- Dimensions: 481 × 113 pixels (RGBA PNG; opaque white background)
- SHA-256: `5d242eac91e95f1cb2ce4f366ce49189c927e1586b55ddf2fbeb61910e3877f5`

The source artwork, colours and aspect ratio are preserved. The logo remains subject to its owner's rights.

## Favicon

`favicon-lightbulb.png` and `favicon.png` (64 × 64) and `apple-touch-icon.png` (180 × 180) display the lightbulb symbol from the official ENLIGHT logo above. `../scripts/render-favicons.cjs` extracts the 108 × 108 region beginning at (8, 2) and resizes it without redrawing the artwork. The original `enlight-logo.png` remains unchanged. The distinct browser-icon filename refreshes the earlier TSCN favicon.

## Social preview

`social-preview.svg` is the locally authored vector composition for the ENLIGHT Checklist Wizard. It displays the lightbulb region of `enlight-logo.png` through an SVG viewport; the original logo file is unchanged. The TSCN Unit mark is reused unchanged. The checklist/document drawing is schematic, not an altered source PDF.

`social-preview.png` is rendered from that layout with `../scripts/render-social-preview.cjs` and Sharp 0.35.5, at 1200 × 630 pixels. Georgia and Arial reproduce the website typography. No AI-generated raster artwork is used.
