About this checklist

ENLIGHT supports reporting of laboratory-based studies on the non-visual effects of light in humans. It is a reporting aid, not a study-quality score.

The official form has 24 reporting rows plus a light-source selection. “Essential” follows the nine reporting rows without a not-applicable option.
